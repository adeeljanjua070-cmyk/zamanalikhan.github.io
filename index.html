About Honorary Captain Zaman Ali Khan

This website preserves the family history and military record of Honorary Captain Zaman Ali Khan, a member of the British Indian Army.

The purpose of this archive is to bring together original family material, official historical records and later research relating to his military service.

The information on this website will be updated as additional historical records are examined and verified.

Family history and documentary evidence are presented separately where appropriate.
